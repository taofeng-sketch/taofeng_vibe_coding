Munch Monster

疯狂大嘴怪

Quick rules: eat fruit, rest your mouth, ignore bombs, then survive the final chaos.
快速玩法：吃水果、闭嘴回体力、别吃炸弹、最后疯狂冲刺。

Eat fruit · Rest mouth · Share score

1. Eat fruit

Open wide when fruit reaches your mouth.

水果靠近嘴巴时张嘴，连续接住会有 combo。

2. Rest

Close your mouth to recharge energy.

一直张嘴会累，闭嘴让能量条恢复。

3. Avoid bombs

Bombs count down and disappear safely if ignored.

炸弹落点倒计时，不吃会安全消失，吃到才会爆。

4. Double time

The last 3 seconds get faster and score double.

最后 3 秒水果和炸弹加速，分数翻倍。
