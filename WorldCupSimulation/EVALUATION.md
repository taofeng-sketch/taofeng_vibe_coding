**Weighted score: 4.35 / 5 (87 / 100)**

## Evaluation tasks

Recruit five football fans and three casual viewers. Ask each person to:

1. Find today's Brazil match and explain who the model favors.
2. Support a team and confirm the choice survives a refresh.
3. Compare Spain and Uruguay, then explain one probability factor.
4. Predict one match and identify whether points have cash value.
5. Play a full penalty round on a phone-sized screen.

## Success metrics

- 90% task completion without facilitator help.
- Median time to first prediction below 45 seconds.
- 100% correctly understand that points have no monetary value.
- At least 4/5 average confidence in understanding probability output.
- Zero critical accessibility or horizontal-overflow defects.
